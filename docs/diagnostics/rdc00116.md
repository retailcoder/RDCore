# RDC00116: Implicit For step

**Severity:** Information &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Family:** `RDC` (Rubberduck Core analyzers) &nbsp;·&nbsp;
**Raised by:** `ForStepAnalyzer`

A `For` loop states no `Step`.

A `For` loop with no `Step` adds 1 to its counter after each iteration
([**MS-VBAL §5.4.2.3** For Statement](../specs/rd-vbal.5.4.2.3.for-statement.md)). Writing `Step 1` states it in the header, next to the bounds.

The diagnostic is reported only when the `Diagnostics.ForStep` setting is `Explicit`. With the default, `Implicit`, a loop is written with no `Step`, and
`Step 1` is [RDC00313](rdc00313.md) instead. Both are legal VBA.

The finding is the header of the loop, up to its end value.

## How it is raised

|Situation|Example|Resolution|
|---|---|---|
|A `For` loop with no `Step`, under the `Explicit` convention.|`For i = 1 To 10`<br>`Next`|Write `Step 1`.|
