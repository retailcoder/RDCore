# RDC00313: Redundant Step 1

**Severity:** Information &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Family:** `RDC` (Rubberduck Core analyzers) &nbsp;·&nbsp;
**Raised by:** `ForStepAnalyzer`

A `For` loop states `Step 1`.

A `For` loop with no `Step` adds 1 to its counter after each iteration
([**MS-VBAL §5.4.2.3** For Statement](../specs/rd-vbal.5.4.2.3.for-statement.md)), so `Step 1` changes nothing.

The diagnostic follows the `Diagnostics.ForStep` setting. With the default, `Implicit`, `Step 1` is reported. With `Explicit`, a loop with no `Step` is
[RDC00116](rdc00116.md) instead. Both are legal VBA.

The finding is the step value.

## How it is raised

|Situation|Example|Resolution|
|---|---|---|
|A `For` loop that states `Step 1`.|`For i = 1 To 10 Step 1`<br>`Next`|Remove `Step 1`.|
